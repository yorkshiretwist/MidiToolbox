MIDI Toolbox

A highly configurable MIDI footswitch controller for musicians whose hands are already busy. It plays notes and chords, changes programs, sends control changes and moves between sets of sounds, all from your feet.

Use it

  • EditorChoose what each button does and save a config file for the unit's SD card.
  • DesignerDesign your own unit: drilling drawing, wiring schematic, parts list and device.json.
  • Desktop appEditor, Designer and USB tools in one download for Windows, Linux and macOS. Installs firmware and configs without the Arduino IDE.

The Editor and Designer run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Build at your own risk

Everything here is provided "as is", with no warranty. Building a unit involves drilling, cutting and soldering: measure your own parts before you drill or cut, and verify your wiring against your parts' datasheets and with a multimeter before you apply power.

About

MIDI Toolbox is a Teensy 4.1 footswitch controller with RGB LEDs and a touchscreen, an open-source firmware and two web apps. Everything is on GitHub, under the GNU General Public License v3.0. It uses third-party software under its own licences.
