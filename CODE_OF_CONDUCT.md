# Code of Conduct

MIDI Toolbox is a small hobby project, and I want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report
problems and share what you have built. This applies to everything connected with the project:
issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where you are representing it.

## What I expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are musicians first and programmers or electronics builders
  second, and everyone started as a beginner.
- Assume good intentions, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Criticism of code, designs and documentation is welcome; criticism
  of the person who wrote them is not.
- Give useful feedback and accept it graciously.
- Respect other people's time: say what you tried, what you expected and what happened.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or trolling.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, age, religion, nationality, appearance, background or level of experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a friendly, professional setting.

## Safety first

Building this unit involves mains-adjacent power supplies, soldering and drilling. Please do not
encourage anyone to do something unsafe, and do not mock people for asking basic safety questions.

## Enforcement

I (the maintainer) am responsible for applying this code. I may edit or remove comments, issues and
pull requests that break it, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose
behaviour I consider harmful. I will try to be fair and to explain my decision.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, you can:

- use GitHub's **Report content** option (the "..." menu on a comment, issue or pull request), which
  tells me and GitHub, or
- open an issue titled "Conduct concern" **without any details**, and I will arrange a private way for
  you to tell me what happened.

Reports are kept private and are treated seriously.
